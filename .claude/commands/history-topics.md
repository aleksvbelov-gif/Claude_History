## WHAT MAKES A GOOD TOPIC

Each topic must be a genuine story — not a biography summary, but a specific episode, turning point, hidden chapter, or overlooked dimension of this person's life. Think of it as identifying the 10 most cinematic scenes from their biography that the general public doesn't know well.

The best topics have at least one of:
- A shocking hidden fact or secret kept for decades
- A dramatic fall from grace, betrayal, or impossible choice
- A private life that contradicts the public image
- A story involving the wrong love, the wrong loyalty, or the wrong place at the wrong time
- A chapter history overlooked or deliberately buried

---

## FOR EACH TOPIC PROVIDE

**1. Title** — punchy, curiosity-driven, under 60 characters. Lead with the tension, not the name.
Examples of good patterns:
- "The Secret [Name] Took to Her Grave"
- "The Woman [Famous Person] Was Forbidden to Love"
- "What [Name] Did When No One Was Watching"
- "The [Event] That Changed Everything"

**2. Hook** — Write the actual opening paragraph of this video (150–200 words). This is not a description of the hook — it IS the hook, written as narration, ready to record.

Follow one of these four approaches:

- **The Final Scene First** — Open at the most dramatic moment. Do not name the person yet. Full sensory detail, then pull back to reveal the larger meaning.
- **The Contradiction** — Establish what the world saw, then immediately undercut it.
- **The Surprising Opening Quote** — A real, verified quote from a diary, letter, or contemporary observer that establishes the central irony.
- **The Impossible Choice** — Open at the moment they had to choose between two lives.

The hook must end with a thematic reframe: *this is not just a story about [event] — it is a story about [deeper human theme].*

Hook style rules:
- Flowing prose, no bullet points
- 1–4 sentences per paragraph, short paragraphs
- Use the staccato triple where it fits: "She was not a soldier. She was not a politician. She was a mother."
- Never tell the viewer how to feel — build to it
- British/neutral English

**3. Angle** — 3–4 sentences explaining what makes this specific story surprising, what the viewer will not expect, and why it works as a standalone video rather than just "part of a biography."

**4. Photo availability** — brief note on what archival photographs, portraits, or press images exist for this specific episode or period of their life.
